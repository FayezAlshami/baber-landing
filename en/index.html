Instagram is a portfolio. Not a booking system.

Most shops still run on DMs, WhatsApp and a phone that rings mid-fade. It works — until it doesn't. Every unanswered message is a chair that stays empty.

No reminder, no deposit, no record. The slot is gone, and so is the revenue you turned down for it.
